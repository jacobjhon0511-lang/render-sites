The Idea Behind It

Binaural beats play a slightly different tone in each ear, and the brain perceives the difference as a slow pulse. The claim is that the brain's electrical rhythm then follows that pulse, a process called entrainment. The pulse is real. Whether it reliably changes mood or thinking is where the evidence is mixed.
