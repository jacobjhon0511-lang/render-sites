potato starch · amount not given

1. Resistant Starch Type 2 (RS2)

A starch that resists digestion and is fermented by gut bacteria.

Studied for: Trials use about 15 to 45 g a day, with modest effects on insulin sensitivity.

Worth knowing: A capsule holds a tiny fraction of that.

prebiotic fibre · amount not given

2. Chicory Root Inulin

A fermentable fibre that feeds bifidobacteria.

Studied for: Typical studies use 5 to 15 g a day; effects on weight are small.

Worth knowing: Can cause gas and bloating.

live bacterium · amount not given

3. Clostridium Butyricum

A butyrate-producing bacterium used as a probiotic, mainly in Asia.

Studied for: Studied for antibiotic-related diarrhoea and gut conditions, not for weight.

Worth knowing: Strain and count are not named.

live bacterium · amount not given

4. Akkermansia Muciniphila

A gut bacterium that lives in the mucus layer.

Studied for: One small human trial of a specific pasteurized preparation reported modest metabolic effects.

Worth knowing: Live capsules are a different product.

live bacterium · amount not given

5. Bifidobacterium Infantis

A bifidobacterium associated with infant guts.

Studied for: Studied for infant gut health and, as another strain, adult IBS.

Worth knowing: No weight-loss trial known.
