How To Read an Herbal Tea Supplement Label

Amounts, servings and claims.

Look for the amount of tea per serving. See safety and interactions.

Amounts

Each herb should have an amount, not just a name. Read the ingredients.

Directions

Steeping time and number of cups should be stated. Read the ingredients.

Claims

Be wary of sexual performance claims on a supplement. Read the ingredients.
