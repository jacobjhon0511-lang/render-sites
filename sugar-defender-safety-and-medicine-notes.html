Sugar Defender Safety and Medicine Notes: Who Should Ask First

A frank look at who may consider it and who should talk to a doctor.

Who May Consider It

Healthy adults aged 18 and over who have read the ingredients, take no interacting medicine and have no blood sugar condition.*

Diabetes Medicine

Gymnema, chromium and ginseng can lower blood sugar, and combining them with diabetes medicine could push it too low. Never change your treatment because of a supplement, and ask your doctor and pharmacist first. See gymnema, chromium and blood sugar supplements and diabetes medicine.

Caffeine

Guarana is a source of caffeine, and no amount is given, so it adds to your coffee and tea. See guarana and guarana and caffeine in supplements.

Blood Pressure and Heart

Coleus, eleuthero and guarana can affect blood pressure and heart rate. See coleus and eleuthero.

Blood Thinners

Ginseng and coleus may add to blood thinners. See ginseng.

Side Effects the Seller Mentions

The seller says some people notice bloating, gas, mild cramps, fatigue, headache or dizziness in the first days. Stop and ask a doctor if symptoms continue. See the FAQ.

Low Blood Sugar Signs

Shakiness, sweating, confusion or a racing heart need prompt attention. See when blood sugar needs a doctor.

Pregnancy, Nursing and Under 18

Ask a doctor first if you are pregnant or nursing, and it is not for anyone under 18. See the FAQ.

Realistic Expectations

No result is promised. See how to read a blood sugar supplement label and the before you order checklist.
