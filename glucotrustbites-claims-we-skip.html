The Claims We Skip, And Why

The seller's page makes claims that the evidence, and the rules for supplements, do not support.*

"Blocks Harmful Resistin Hormones"

Resistin is a hormone studied in animals and people, but its role in human blood sugar is still debated, and no trial shows these ingredients lower it. See resistin, what we know.

See also: Ingredients · Reviews

"Improves Insulin Sensitivity"

Some single ingredients show small effects in small trials. That is not a result for this chew.

See also: Reviews · FAQ

"For People Who Struggle With High Blood Sugar"

That is a condition claim. A supplement may not claim to treat high blood sugar or diabetes.

See also: FAQ · How To Judge Blood Sugar Supplement Claims

"Sublingual Absorption Technology"

A chewable tablet is not a proven faster way to absorb these ingredients. See chewable and sublingual claims.

See also: How To Judge Blood Sugar Supplement Claims · Ingredients

"Thousands Of Happy Users", "98.7% Order 6 Bottles"

None of these numbers can be checked.
