How to read it: the Daily Value is "not established" for every line. All eight amounts are printed, which is a plus, and the total of the active amounts is about 700 mg.

Directions (label): take one capsule daily with an 8 oz glass of water, for best results 20 to 30 minutes before a meal, or as directed by your healthcare professional. Do not exceed the recommended dose. Store in a cool, dry place away from heat and light. Keep out of reach of children.

Label amount: 500 mg

1. Glucosamine Sulfate

An amino sugar the body makes for cartilage, sold here from shellfish shells.

Studied for: Joint comfort trials, mixed, at about 1,500 mg a day.

Worth knowing: Contains shellfish, may affect warfarin and blood sugar; no nerve evidence.

Label amount: 67 mg

2. Indian Frankincense (Boswellia serrata)

A tree resin whose boswellic acids are studied for joint comfort.

Studied for: Small joint trials at 100 to 250 mg or more of standardized extract.

Worth knowing: May upset the stomach; no nerve evidence.

Label amount: 50 mg

3. Chondroitin Sulfate

A cartilage building block, often paired with glucosamine.

Studied for: Joint trials use about 800 to 1,200 mg a day.

Worth knowing: Here it is about 4% of that; may add to blood thinners.

Label amount: 50 mg

4. Turmeric Root

The golden root behind curry, source of curcumin.

Studied for: Curcumin trials use hundreds of mg of curcumin with absorption aids.

Worth knowing: 50 mg of root powder holds only a small amount of curcumin.

Label amount: 8 mg

5. Quercetin (Sophora japonica)

A plant flavonoid in onions and apples.

Studied for: Mostly lab work and small trials at hundreds of mg.

Worth knowing: 8 mg is a food-sized amount.

Label amount: 8 mg

6. Methionine

An essential sulfur amino acid found in protein foods.

Studied for: A normal diet supplies grams.

Worth knowing: 8 mg is trivial next to food.

Label amount: 8 mg

7. MSM (Methylsulfonylmethane)

An organic sulfur compound used for joint comfort.

Studied for: Joint trials use 1,500 to 3,000 mg.

Worth knowing: 8 mg is under 1% of that.

Label amount: 8 mg

8. Bromelain (Pineapple Stem)

A mix of enzymes from pineapple stems.

Studied for: Swelling and joint trials use hundreds of mg.

Worth knowing: May affect bleeding and antibiotic absorption.
