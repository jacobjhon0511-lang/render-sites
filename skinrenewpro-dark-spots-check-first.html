Dark Spots: Get Them Checked First

A new or changing dark spot is a reason to see a doctor, not to buy a cream.*

See A Doctor Or Dermatologist For A Spot That

  • Is new, or changing in size, shape or colour
  • Has an uneven border or several colours
  • Is larger than a pencil eraser, itches, bleeds or will not heal
  • Looks different from your other spots

See also: Sun protection first · Skin Claims We Skip

Why

Most dark patches are harmless sun spots or melasma, but a few can be skin cancer, and early checks matter.

See also: Skin Claims We Skip · FAQ

What A Cream Cannot Do

A moisturizer is not shown to fade spots, and the seller's page says it is not a treatment for a skin condition. See sun protection first and the claims we skip.
