Burntide Common Questions

What is Burntide?

An apple-flavored gummy with a 525 mg blend of apple cider vinegar and BHB calcium, magnesium and sodium salts, marketed to support healthy weight management.

What does the label list?

A 2.5 g gummy, 30 servings, 8 calories, 2 g sugars (1 g added), 2.5 mg sodium, 1 mg calcium, 0.75 mg magnesium and a 525 mg proprietary blend.

How do I take it?

One gummy daily, about 30 minutes before a morning or afternoon meal, with a full glass of water.

Is it a real fat burner?

No. The evidence does not support that, and no weight-loss result is promised.

Does it contain sugar?

Yes: 2 g of sugars per gummy, including 1 g of added sugar, from corn syrup and cane sugar.

Is it safe with diabetes?

Ask first. The sugar, the vinegar and the ketone salts can all matter, above all with insulin or SGLT2 inhibitors.

Is it FDA approved?

No. Supplements are not. The seller only says it is made in an FDA-registered facility.

Is it stimulant-free?

The seller says it contains no caffeine or stimulants.

What does it cost?

Is it a subscription?

The seller says no: a one-time purchase with no rebills.

What is the guarantee?

A 60-day money-back guarantee, and the seller says empty bottles are accepted.

When should I see a doctor?

For nausea with deep fast breathing, confusion, severe belly pain, vomiting, or swelling or trouble breathing.
