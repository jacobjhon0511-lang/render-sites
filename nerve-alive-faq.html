Nerve Alive Questions and Answers

What is Nerve Alive?

A capsule dietary supplement for adults, 60 capsules per bottle, positioned to support nerve comfort and everyday mobility. See about Nerve Alive.

What ingredients does it name?

Benfotiamine, Methylcobalamin, Alpha-Lipoic Acid, Acetyl-L-Carnitine, Magnesium Glycinate, Turmeric, Capsaicin, Passionflower and Feverfew. See the Nerve Alive ingredients.

Are the amounts listed?

Not on the seller's page, which describes the formula as a proprietary blend. Check the Supplement Facts on your bottle, and do not rely on a figure you cannot see on the label.

How do I take Nerve Alive?

Two capsules daily with water, preferably with a meal, never more. Some marketing material shows one capsule a day, so follow your bottle label. See how Nerve Alive works.

Does it treat neuropathy?

No. It is a dietary supplement and does not diagnose, treat or cure neuropathy, diabetes or any condition. See what nerve supplements cannot do.

I have tingling or numb feet. Should I just try it?

Please see a doctor first. Tingling and numbness can have causes that need testing, such as low vitamin B12 or high blood sugar. See tingling and numb feet: when to see a doctor.

Is it safe with my medicines?

Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, especially about diabetes, blood-thinning, thyroid, seizure and sedative medicines. See supplements and medicines for nerve health.

How soon will I notice anything?

No timeline is promised and results vary. The seller suggests three to six months of daily use before judging, which is longer than the 60-day guarantee, so plan with that in mind.
