How Does Nail Defender Work?

A plain walkthrough based on the product's own directions.

1

Spray Onto Clean, Dry Nails

Apply a light mist directly onto clean, dry nails and the surrounding skin as part of your routine.

2

Let The Botanical Blend Settle

The blend of oils and aloe vera leaf extract is left to settle onto the nail and skin surface before covering with socks or shoes.

3

Repeat Daily For Consistency

Nail care habits work best as a routine. Consistent daily use is part of how the product is intended to be incorporated into grooming.

Consistency matters more than quantity: applying more product or using it more often than directed does not necessarily improve results. Follow the directions on your bottle for the best experience.
