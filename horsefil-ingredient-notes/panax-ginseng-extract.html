About Panax Ginseng Extract

In short: A famous root in traditional medicine.*

Panax ginseng is one of the best-known herbs in traditional medicine, taken for stamina and vitality. The seller lists it last.

See the ingredients and safety and interactions.

A Famous Root

Panax ginseng has been used for centuries in East Asian medicine. See panax ginseng: benefits and cautions.

What Research Shows

Studies on fatigue and stamina are mixed and modest. See safety and interactions.

Main Caution

Ginseng can affect blood sugar, blood pressure and blood thinners and can disturb sleep in some people. Check with your doctor and pharmacist first. See safety and interactions.
