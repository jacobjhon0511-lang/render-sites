When To See A Doctor, Not A Listening Program

An audio program should never stand in for care.*

See A Doctor For

  • Sleep problems lasting weeks
  • Persistent low mood, worry or panic
  • Poor focus that affects work or school
  • New ringing in the ears or hearing change

See also: Mental health and sleep · Seizures and epilepsy

Seek Emergency Care For

  • A seizure or blackout
  • Thoughts of harming yourself or others
  • Chest pain or trouble breathing

See also: Seizures and epilepsy · FAQ

Why

Treatable causes lie behind many of these problems, and a program cannot find them.
