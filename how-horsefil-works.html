What the gummy is, how it is taken, and what to expect.

What the Gummy Does

HorseFil is a supplement and not a drug. It combines three botanical extracts and two amino acids in a gummy, and the jar positions it to support energy, vitality and performance.* See the ingredients and the benefits.

Four Steps

Follow these with the daily routine beside you.

1

Read the jar

Study the directions, amounts and cautions on your jar.

2

Ask about medicine

Show your pharmacist the ingredient list if you take any medicine.

3

One gummy

The seller describes one gummy per day.

4

Stay consistent

Judge it over weeks, beside training and sleep.

Tips

Small habits make it stick. See men's energy daily habits.

  • Take it at the same time each day.
  • Do not take more than the jar says.
  • Store it as the label directs, away from heat.
  • Keep it away from children, since it tastes like candy.
