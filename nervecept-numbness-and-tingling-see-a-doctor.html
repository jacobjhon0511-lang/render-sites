Numbness, Tingling And Burning: See A Doctor First

A supplement should not be the first response to these symptoms.*

Why A Diagnosis Matters

Burning, tingling, numbness and weakness in the hands and feet can come from diabetes, vitamin B12 deficiency, thyroid disease, alcohol, medicines, pinched nerves, kidney disease and other causes, some of them quickly treatable.

See also: Diabetes and blood sugar · A Joint Formula, A Nerve Label

See A Doctor Soon For

  • Numbness or tingling that spreads or lasts more than a few weeks
  • Burning that wakes you at night
  • Weak grip, dropping things or tripping
  • Open sores or injuries you cannot feel

See also: A Joint Formula, A Nerve Label · FAQ

Seek Emergency Care For

  • Sudden weakness or numbness on one side, a drooping face or trouble speaking
  • Loss of bladder or bowel control, or numbness in the groin
  • Sudden severe weakness in the legs
