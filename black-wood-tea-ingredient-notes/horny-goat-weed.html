About Horny Goat Weed

In short: Traditional vitality herb, named first.*

Horny goat weed (Epimedium) is a leafy herb long used in traditional Chinese practice, and its best-known compound is icariin. The seller names it first and calls it a cornerstone of the blend.

See the ingredients and safety and interactions.

Where It Grows

A leafy herb of East Asian and Mediterranean hillsides. See horny goat weed and icariin: what to know.

What Research Shows

Most research is in animals or cells, and human data is limited. See the benefits.

Main Caution

Epimedium can affect blood pressure and heart rhythm in some people and may interact with blood pressure medicine, nitrates and blood thinners. Ask a doctor first if you have a heart condition. See safety and interactions.
