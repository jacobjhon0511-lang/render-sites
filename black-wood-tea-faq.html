Black Wood Tea FAQ: Ingredients, Safety, Price and Refunds

Straight answers to what people ask most.

What is Black Wood Tea?

Black Wood Tea is an herbal tea supplement sold in a pouch for men who want to support healthy blood flow, energy and endurance.* The pouch calls it a herbal tea supplement. It is not a medicine and not a treatment for erectile dysfunction or any other condition.

What ingredients does it list?

The seller names eight: horny goat weed, ginseng, maca root, tongkat ali, ginkgo biloba, tribulus terrestris, catuaba bark and L-arginine, with no amounts. See the ingredients.

Is it a tea or a pill?

The seller calls it a tea and the pouch photos show a tea bag-style herbal tea supplement, although some of the seller's alt text says "pills" and "bottles". Check the product and directions on the pouch you receive.

How do I prepare it?

The seller says it is easy to prepare and describes drinking it as part of a daily routine, without giving steps or a serving size. Follow the directions printed on your pouch, and do not exceed them.

Does it treat erectile dysfunction?

No. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, including erectile dysfunction. Erection problems can be an early sign of heart or blood sugar problems, so see a doctor.

Who should talk to a doctor first?

Anyone with heart disease, low or high blood pressure or diabetes, anyone who takes nitrates, blood pressure medicine, blood thinners or erectile dysfunction medicine, and anyone pregnant, nursing or under 18. See safety and interactions.

Can it interact with my medicines?

Yes, possibly. L-arginine and horny goat weed can interact with nitrates and erectile dysfunction drugs, and ginkgo can add to blood thinners, so show the label to your pharmacist first.

Why do the seller's pages mention ingredients that are not on the list?

The seller's benefits text also mentions yohimbe, rhodiola, bacopa and beetroot, which are not in its ingredient list. Confirm the real formula on the pouch, and avoid any product that contains yohimbe without medical advice.

What does it cost?

The seller says the regular price is $99 and today's price is $49 a pouch, with 1, 3 and 6 pouch options and one-time payments. See pricing and confirm every total at checkout.

How does the guarantee work?

It is a 60-day money-back guarantee. The seller says to contact support by email within 60 days of purchase. See the 60-day guarantee and the refund policy.

Are there bonuses?

The seller lists two free bonus ebooks with the 3 and 6 pouch packs. One cover is shown here, and you should confirm the list at checkout.

How long will shipping take?

The seller says it ships by UPS, processes orders within 24 hours and delivers in 5 to 7 business days. Confirm this at checkout.

Is Black Wood Tea FDA approved?

No. The FDA does not approve dietary supplements, and this site shows no "FDA Approved" badge. The seller says it is made in an FDA-registered facility, and registration is not approval.

Why are there no star ratings or named customers here?

Named, "verified" reviews with cities, personal claims and photos cannot be checked from here. This site shows plain themes only.
