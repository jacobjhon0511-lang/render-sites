Safety

Patch Test First, Every Time

If you read one safety page, read this one.*

How To Patch Test

  1. Wash and dry a small area, such as the inner forearm or behind the ear
  2. Apply a small dab of cream and leave it
  3. Check after 24 hours, and again at 48 hours
  4. If there is redness, itching, swelling or a rash, do not use it

See also: Nut and plant allergies · Eczema, rosacea, sensitive skin

Why

Plant extracts and nut-derived ingredients can cause allergic or irritant reactions, and a patch test shows a reaction on a small area first.

See also: Eczema, rosacea, sensitive skin · FAQ

If You React

Wash it off with cool water and stop. Seek urgent care for facial swelling or trouble breathing.
