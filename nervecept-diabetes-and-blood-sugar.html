Diabetes, Blood Sugar And Nervecept

Diabetes is one of the commonest causes of nerve symptoms, so this page matters twice.*

The Supplement

Glucosamine has been studied for effects on blood sugar, and results are mixed and mostly reassuring, but people with diabetes are usually advised to check their levels more closely when starting it.

The Symptoms

If you have diabetes and new burning, numbness or foot sores, see your doctor. Good glucose control, foot care and proper treatment matter far more than any capsule.
