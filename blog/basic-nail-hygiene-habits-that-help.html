Basic Nail Hygiene Habits That Help

A product is one piece of a nail-care routine. Here are the everyday habits that matter alongside it.

Keep Nails Clean And Dry

Moisture trapped around nails is one of the most common everyday issues — drying nails and surrounding skin thoroughly after washing or bathing is a simple, foundational habit.

Use Clean Grooming Tools

Clippers, files, and other tools that touch multiple nails are worth keeping clean and, where possible, dedicated to personal use only.

Choose Breathable Footwear And Socks

Where relevant, breathable materials reduce the amount of time nails spend in a warm, enclosed environment throughout the day.
