Protecting Your Hearing: Daily Habits That Help

A supplement is one piece of a broader picture. Here are everyday habits that support hearing health alongside your routine.

Manage Noise Exposure

Prolonged exposure to loud noise is one of the most preventable contributors to hearing changes over time — simple steps like lowering volume and using ear protection matter.

Stay Hydrated And Active

General cardiovascular health supports circulation throughout the body, including to the ears — the same circulation angle ingredients like Ginkgo Biloba are included to support.

Get Regular Check-Ups

Routine hearing checks can catch changes early, giving you more options and context than waiting until a noticeable problem develops.
