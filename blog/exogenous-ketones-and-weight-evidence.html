Exogenous Ketones And Weight: The Evidence

Does drinking ketones help you lose weight?

What Trials Show

Studies of ketone salts and esters focus on exercise and blood ketones. Reviews find no reliable weight-loss benefit from taking ketones on their own.

See also: Ingredients · Diabetes and ketones

Why Not

Weight loss follows energy balance over time, and supplement ketones add calories and minerals rather than removing body fat. See weight basics after 30.
