Circulation And Hearing: The Connection

Several of EchoXen's ingredients are included specifically to support circulation. Here's why that matters for hearing.

The Ear Needs A Steady Blood Supply

Like any tissue in the body, the delicate structures of the inner ear rely on a steady supply of oxygen and nutrients delivered through blood flow.

How Reduced Circulation Affects Hearing

When circulation to the ear is reduced, auditory nerves may not receive what they need to function optimally, which can contribute to hearing changes over time.
