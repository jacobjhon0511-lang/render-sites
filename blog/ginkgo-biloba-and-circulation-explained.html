Ginkgo Biloba & Circulation Explained

Ginkgo Biloba is one of the most recognized botanicals in circulation-focused formulas, and it's the first ingredient listed in EchoXen's formula.

A Long History Of Use

Ginkgo Biloba is an ancient herbal extract long studied for its role in supporting healthy blood circulation, including to the ears and brain.

Why Circulation Matters For Hearing

Healthy blood flow helps deliver oxygen and nutrients to the auditory nerves, which may help protect against further damage and support hearing clarity over time.

How It Fits Into EchoXen's Formula

Ginkgo Biloba works alongside Magnesium, Zinc, Vitamin B12 and Alpha Lipoic Acid in EchoXen's five-ingredient blend — see our Zinc & Magnesium breakdown for two more.
