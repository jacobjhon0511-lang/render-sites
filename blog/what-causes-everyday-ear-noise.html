What Causes Everyday Ear Noise

Ringing, buzzing or humming in the ears is one of the most common everyday complaints. Here's a closer look at what commonly contributes to it.

Poor Circulation

Reduced blood flow to the delicate tissues of the ear can affect how well auditory nerves receive the oxygen and nutrients they need — one reason circulation-focused ingredients like Ginkgo Biloba are common in this category.

Inflammation

Inflammation inside the ear can contribute to ringing, muffled hearing, and general discomfort, which is why anti-inflammatory support is often paired with circulation support.

Nerve Stress

Auditory nerves under stress — from noise exposure, aging, or nutritional gaps — may not transmit sound signals as effectively, contributing to perceived noise or clarity issues.
