HorseFil Ingredients: Maca, Rhodiola, Citrulline and More

The six named ingredients, and the main caution for each.

The seller names six ingredients: maca root extract, rhodiola extract, grape seed extract, L-citrulline, L-carnitine and panax ginseng extract, with no amounts.* The seller's headline says eight, so confirm the real formula on your jar. Each note covers what the item is, what research exists and who should ask first, and the home page has a tap-through version.

Maca Root Extract in HorseFil

In short: Traditional Andean root, named first.*

Maca is a radish-family root from the Peruvian Andes, eaten as food and sold as a powder or extract. The seller lists it first and describes it as a non-stimulant root used traditionally for endurance and men's wellness.

More on maca root extract · safety and interactions

Rhodiola Extract in HorseFil

In short: An adaptogenic herb with some stimulating effect.*

Rhodiola (Rhodiola rosea) is an arctic root traditionally used to help the body cope with physical and mental stress. The seller lists it second.

More on rhodiola extract · safety and interactions

Grape Seed Extract in HorseFil

In short: Mainly an antioxidant.*

Grape seed extract is a source of polyphenols, and the seller describes it as a supporting-cast antioxidant for active people. It is listed third.

More on grape seed extract · safety and interactions

L-Citrulline in HorseFil

In short: The best-researched ingredient on the list.*

L-citrulline is an amino acid that the body converts into arginine and then nitric oxide, a molecule that relaxes blood vessels. Of the six names, it has the most human research on circulation and exercise.

More on l-citrulline · safety and interactions

L-Carnitine in HorseFil

In short: Helps move fat into the cell's energy centres.*

L-carnitine is made in the body and carries fatty acids into the mitochondria, the cell's energy centres. It is sold for energy and exercise recovery.

More on l-carnitine · safety and interactions

Panax Ginseng Extract in HorseFil

In short: A famous root in traditional medicine.*

Panax ginseng is one of the best-known herbs in traditional medicine, taken for stamina and vitality. The seller lists it last.

More on panax ginseng extract · safety and interactions
