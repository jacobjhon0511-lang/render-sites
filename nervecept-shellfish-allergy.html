Shellfish Allergy And Nervecept

If you read one safety page, read this one.*

What The Label Says

"CONTAINS: SHELLFISH (CRAYFISH)". Glucosamine is commonly made from shellfish shells.

Who Must Not Take It

Anyone with a shellfish allergy. Reactions can include hives, swelling, vomiting and trouble breathing.

If You Are Unsure

  • Ask your doctor or allergist before starting
  • Remember that some people react to shrimp or crab but tolerate other foods
  • Do not rely on the idea that processing removes the protein
