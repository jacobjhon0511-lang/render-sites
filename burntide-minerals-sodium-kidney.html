Minerals, Sodium And Kidney Health

The amounts on the panel are tiny, but the principle is worth knowing.*

What The Panel Shows

Who Should Still Ask

  • People with kidney disease, who must watch minerals
  • People on strict low-sodium diets or with heart failure
  • People taking diuretics, digoxin or potassium-affecting medicines
